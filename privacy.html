Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This site is the personal portfolio of Akinyemi Fiyinfoluwa. This policy explains what information the site handles and what happens to it.

What this site collects

The site has no accounts, comments, advertising or analytics, and it sets no cookies. It has a contact form. When you send it, your name, email address and message are passed through a third-party form delivery service to my inbox. Nothing is sent until you press the send button. I do not otherwise collect personal information from visitors.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served by a hosting provider. Like most web hosts, the provider may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operation of the service. That data is handled under the provider's own privacy statement, and I do not use it.

Contact form delivery

The form posts your name, email address and message over HTTPS to an email delivery service, which forwards them to me. That service processes the data on my behalf and under its own privacy statement. If the service cannot be reached, the form offers a link to email me directly instead.

Fonts

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

If you email me

If you write to inventorfiyin.2022@gmail.com, I receive your email address and the contents of your message. I use them only to reply and to continue the conversation. I keep the correspondence for as long as it is useful and delete it on request.

Links to other sites

The site links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, Credly and other external services. Those services have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how I use it. Email me and I will respond. If you are in Nigeria, you also have rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting anything new, I will update this page and change the effective date above.
